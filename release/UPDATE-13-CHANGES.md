## 1. A monthly "how is your memory doing" summary

About once a month — the first time you open the Review screen in a new
month — Memory_OB shows one short card at the top:

> **Monthly check-up**
> 264 memories and notes are saved, about 31 people.
> This month: 12 new, 4 corrected, 2 put aside.
> 3 things to come back to, 1 of them past its date.
> Nothing is waiting for your answer.

It shows **once**, then waits its turn again — it is there to glance at, not
to nag. You can also ask for it any time: **Settings → "How is my memory
doing?"** always answers.

This is separate from the nightly check-up that looks for problems. This one
is the friendly "how are things going" note.
