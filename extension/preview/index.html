FIELD NOTES / EVERYDAY PLACES

A small reading room,
with room to think.

The useful parts of a place are not always the things you notice first.

At the back of the library, six chairs face a long window. There is a shelf for books people are still reading, a table for unfinished notes, and a lamp someone remembered to repair.

A good reading room makes it easy to return. Keep the tools within reach. Leave enough space for an open notebook. Put the books back where the next person will look.
