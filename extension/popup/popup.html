What may be collected

Messaging and Classroom need one-time site access. Readers only collect opened conversations or course streams, not inbox-wide history, Facebook posts or Instagram feeds. Reload existing tabs after enabling a site.

AI sites and capture coverage

Readers depend on the site's current layout. Unrecognized speakers or message identities are flagged. Attachments that cannot be downloaded remain pending, never counted as saved. Rendered images may be previews rather than full-resolution originals. Transfers are limited to 256 MB per attachment. No automatic transcription or external AI processing.

Browser history is not collected. Existing saved visits stay in your vault. Manual saves work on the tab you choose without broad access. Private windows are disabled. Reload open tabs after changing site access.
